FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the studio open? +

We're open Monday to Saturday, 10AM – 9PM. Sessions can start on the hour from 10:00, and must finish by 21:00.

How do I book a studio? +

Create a free account, pick a studio and click Book. Choose a session type, duration, date and time, then pay securely with Paystack. You can track every booking from your dashboard.

What are your rates? +

Each studio has an hourly rate shown on its card. An in-house session engineer can be added for GH₵100 per hour. Your total is shown before you pay.

Can I cancel a booking? +

Yes — you can cancel a pending or approved upcoming booking from your dashboard. The slot is released immediately. Contact us about refunds.

Can I use instruments without booking them? +

Yes. All gear listed on the site is available during your session — just let us know what you need in the booking notes.

Can I bring my own engineer or producer? +

Absolutely. You're welcome to bring your own engineer or producer — you only pay for the room.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept card, bank transfer and mobile money via Paystack. Payment is processed securely when you book.
