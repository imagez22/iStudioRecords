FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the studio open on weekends? +

Yes, we're open Monday - Saturday a week, from 10AM to 9PM

How do I book a studio? +

Click the 'Book Now' button to book directly through our dashboard

Can I use instruments without booking? +

Yes, we require you choose the instruments physically at the time of appointment

What are your rates for studio booking? +

Our rates vary by studio type and duration. Please contact us for detailed pricing information.

Do you provide mixing and mastering services? +

Yes, we offer professional mixing and mastering services with experienced engineers.

Can I bring my own engineer or producer? +

Absolutely. You're welcome to bring your own engineer or producer. We charge only for the room, not the engineer.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major payment methods via Paystack — card, bank transfer, and USSD. Payment is processed securely at booking.
